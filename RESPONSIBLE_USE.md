# Responsible use

**For understanding and good-faith communication. Never for deception, coercion, or exploiting vulnerabilities. Respect the other person's right to disagree.**

## The author's appeal

The following passage is retained from Eugene Prudchenko's approved article:

> I sincerely ask you, from the bottom of my heart, **to use these methods only for good.**
>
> In my own experience, abusing such techniques can turn against the person using them. But honest treatment matters for more than the consequences you may face. Another person deserves respect whether or not they can recognize pressure and protect themselves.
>
> Please do not mistake this methodology for NLP, mind-reading, the "programming" of people, or a guarantee of a desired reaction. Our assumptions may be wrong. Persuasiveness does not give anyone the right to conceal material facts. A refusal is not an obstacle to be quietly bypassed.
>
> The responsibility for good-faith communication lies with the person using the method, not with the recipient's ability to resist it.
>
> Warnings alone cannot prevent misuse. That is why limits must be reflected in the instructions, examples, and evaluations, not just in an appeal from the author.
>
> I want readers to leave these protocols thinking not "Now I know how to control people," but **"Now I better understand how to communicate, and where I must stop."**
>
> **Strong communication helps someone make a decision. It should never take that decision away from them.**

## What official outputs must not do

Do not conceal material scope, fees, obligations or risks; fabricate evidence or approval; exploit illness, dependency, fear or distress; make group-based or appearance-based character judgments; imply scientific validation that does not exist; turn silence into consent; or hide a legitimate path to disagree.

Firm, truthful advocacy is allowed. Analysis of an abusive message to correct or challenge it is allowed. A legitimate refusal, objection or request for verification must not trigger blanket refusal by the assistant.

The reference model runner has no send, purchase, submission, browser or account-access tool. Portable skills do not grant permissions to other tools. Host security controls must still restrict external actions.

## How to review a result

Read the recipient-facing text, not only the system's self-declared safety fields. Check the important conditions against the actual input. Look for newly invented authority, pressure, misrepresentation and unsupported claims. Do not treat a schema pass or an ethical notice as proof of good faith.

If the result is abusive or materially false, reject it even if every field is populated. Record the example with synthetic or safely redacted data and describe why it failed. Do not publish confidential material in a public issue.

These are intended-use and output rules, not a guarantee of enforcement over third-party copies. They do not substitute for the owner's separate public-licensing decision.
