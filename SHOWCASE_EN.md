### Letter to Sofya — editorial English translation

Sofya, hello!

I propose approving an order for three in-person sessions on editing exhibition labels. The work centres on accuracy and preserving the author's voice while making the text clearer.

During the sessions, employees will work with six anonymised labels provided by the museum: identify claims about the object, examine which source supports them, and prepare proposed edits. I suggest assessing each edit against three questions: does it preserve the original meaning, does the claim stay within what the source supports, and is the author's voice preserved? If support is unclear, that is a reason to flag a question for verification, rather than strengthen the claim for the sake of simplicity.

Order terms:

- Three sessions: 5, 12 and 19 November 2026, from 14:00 to 15:30, for one group of up to 24 employees.
- Total fixed price: 54,000 roubles for all three sessions. Preparation, the sessions and a PDF of exercises are included; there are no additional materials charges.
- The museum provides the room, screen and six anonymised labels for the work.
- Recording the sessions is not included in the offer. The supplied materials may be used internally at the museum for six months; public redistribution is not permitted.

The purpose of the sessions is to examine the links between the object, the claim and its supporting source, and prepare proposed edits. I do not promise increased attendance or a measured training effect.

Please confirm approval of the order on these terms by replying to this email. If any term does not suit you, indicate it in your reply.

Ilya

## 2. Reverse: an invitation before and after

### Original draft — editorial English translation

Lydia, hello! We invite your club to become a partner in our new programme at the River House. Everything is already prepared; all we need from you are photographs and confirmation. We will hold the meeting on 14 November; we expect guests towards the evening. We can announce it to participants straight away.

### Revised invitation — editorial English translation

Lydia, hello!

We propose that you lead one meeting of your local-history club at the River House on 14 November 2026, from 18:00 to 20:00. You choose the topic. This invitation concerns only that meeting and creates no obligations for the whole programme.

The meeting will take place in the reading room at 12 Sadovaya Street, entrance from the courtyard. Organisers can enter the room from 17:30. There are 30 places in total, of which up to 12 are allocated to your club's participants. Guest participation is free. We offer you a fee of 6,000 roubles for leading the meeting; the organiser bears the costs.

Three photographs that you have the right to use are sufficient for the display in the room. Permission to show them will not extend to publishing them online: that would require your separate permission.

There is a projector in the room, but the ability to display the TIFF files you sent has not yet been checked. Pavel, the technical coordinator, is checking this; the timing of his reply is unknown. We therefore cannot currently confirm that the display is technically ready.

Anton, a member of the River House staff, will organise registration, seating and announcements. We will publish the announcement only after you explicitly agree to lead this meeting. If your decision depends on the file check, please say so in your reply: we will not treat such a reply as final agreement or as permission to announce the event.

Please let us know by 2 November 2026 whether you are willing to lead the meeting on these terms, or what needs clarifying for your decision. If that deadline is inconvenient, suggest another one. You may also decline; we will not treat no response as consent.

Thank you!
