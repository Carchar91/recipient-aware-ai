## Channel-specific findings

- **Hacker News:** current [HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html) prohibit generated/AI-edited posts and comments and automated posting. The prepared material is a human-authoring brief. Eugene must independently write final copy; do not paste this generated material into HN. The [Show HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) also require something users can try, rather than only an article or landing page.
- **Article chronology:** the live article still describes the GitHub specification and plugin as intended work. The [proposed dated update](../distribution/ARTICLE_UPDATE.md) distinguishes the now-existing rc.2 release from a plugin that is not publicly listed; the website is unchanged.
- **About/topics/social preview:** only [proposals and an art brief](../distribution/GITHUB_PRESENTATION.md) are prepared. No settings mutation or image upload is implied by the Draft PR.
- **Community examples:** [the post](../distribution/DEVELOPER_COMMUNITY.md) labels the two revised sentences as separate excerpts from editorial translations. It neither invents a new model answer nor claims a real recipient reaction.
- **Paid outreach:** [the optional addendum](../distribution/PITCH_ADDENDUM.md) is a distinct, unsent item. Preparation does not widen an existing media assignment or authorise contacting anyone.
